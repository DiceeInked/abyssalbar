Keyboard shortcuts

R starts or finishes a take. Space pauses or resumes. D selects the pen. E selects the eraser. H selects the highlighter. Ctrl/⌘ + Z undoes the last drawing. Enter finishes a recording. Shortcuts are disabled while typing in text fields.
