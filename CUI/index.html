Where CUI is going

Stage 01 · Complete

Creator workspace

Browser recording, drawing tools, keyboard shortcuts, synchronized visual capture, and local video export.

Stage 02 · Partial

Speech intelligence

Browser speech recognition, sentence cues, transcript editing, and linking sketches to spoken moments. Accurate word-level alignment and semantic AI analysis remain future work.

Keyboard shortcuts

R starts or finishes a take. Space pauses or resumes. D selects the pen. E selects the eraser. H selects the highlighter. Ctrl/⌘ + Z undoes the last drawing. Enter finishes a recording. Shortcuts are disabled while typing in text fields.
