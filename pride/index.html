What the words mean

    One combination doesn’t always have one special name. The result combines existing terms where useful. If no widely used term describes a presentation preference or circumstance, it will say so instead of inventing a label.

    Words are tools, not boxes. You can use more than one, change what you call yourself later, or decide that no label fits.
