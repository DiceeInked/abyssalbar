Each color's own rules

A row describes how that color moves in response to another color. The reverse direction is a separate rule.

Responding dot ↓RedGreenBlueYellow
Strong attraction moves the row's dots toward the column's dots. Repulsion makes the row's dots move away. No response means this rule adds no acceleration. A dot never directly pushes another dot, and there is no automatic collision-push.

Size and distance per color

Distances are in simulated centimeters. Rules act between the inner and outer distance, then fade smoothly to zero at either edge. These are virtual units, not a measurement of your physical screen.
